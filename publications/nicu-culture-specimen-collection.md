## Overview

An educational resource for NICU nurses covering safe neonatal
culture-specimen collection, contamination prevention, specimen
labeling and transport, documentation, and procedural comfort.

## Clinical Safety

Educational purposes only. Follow current evidence-based guidance,
institutional protocols, laboratory requirements, and professional
scope of practice. Specimen collection must not delay urgently
indicated treatment for suspected neonatal sepsis.
